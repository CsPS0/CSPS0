<details>
  <summary>🤔 Why did I choose programming?</summary>
  <br />
  My interest in IT didn't start just because I was a gamer at a young age; it started because I was always fascinated by how technical things actually work under the hood. I loved exploring and using various open-source tools, always digging deeper than the surface-level stuff you'd find on YouTube, all while steering clear of piracy. I officially started learning programming in 8th grade when I was about 12 years old. Back then, there were no AI or LLMs in sight, and I also saw software development as a great career path with a strong job market. However, just one year later in 9th grade, the coding landscape completely shifted and the entry-level market felt like it collapsed with the release of ChatGPT 3.5. Realizing how rapidly AI can change everything, I decided to broaden my skill set. As a result, I started learning about computer networks as well to build a more resilient and versatile foundation in IT.
</details>
